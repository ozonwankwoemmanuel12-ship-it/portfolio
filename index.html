About Me

20+ Repos & counting

I'm a frontend developer who loves turning ideas into interfaces people actually enjoy using. My work lives at the intersection of clean code, thoughtful design and real-world accessibility.

I started with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and grew into component-driven development with React and utility-first styling with Tailwind CSS. From calculators and quote machines to e-commerce frontends and weather apps, I ship projects end to end: structure, style, logic and deployment.

I care about websites that load fast, work on every screen size and stay usable with a keyboard or screen reader — because good development should work for everyone.
